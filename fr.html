About Us

A Research Platform for Sustainable Development.

EcoResearch Association brings together researchers, academics, economists, and experts committed to producing and disseminating knowledge that informs public policy, supports the private sector, and contributes to the advancement of civil society.

The Association contributes to strengthening scientific and policy dialogue on the challenges of economic transformation, social inclusion, governance, innovation, and resilience across Africa.

Vision

To become a leading African center of excellence in economic research, public policy analysis, and scientific knowledge dissemination.

Mission

To produce rigorous analyses, publish high-quality scientific research, and support decision-makers in designing evidence-based policies.

Research Areas

Areas of Expertise

EcoResearch addresses the key contemporary challenges of economic development and public policy.

01

Governance and institutional economics

Institutional quality, public policy effectiveness, transparency, local governance, and natural resource management.

02

Structural transformation

Industrialization, economic diversification, competitiveness, value chain development, and regional integration.

03

Development finance

Public debt, taxation, domestic resource mobilization, infrastructure, and sustainable finance.

04

Innovation and the digital economy

Artificial intelligence, finTech, digital transformation, innovation, and digital sovereignty.

05

Climate and Energy

Energy transition, energy access, climate resilience, climate-smart agriculture, and sustainable development.

06

Human capital and inclusion

Education, youth employment, social protection, inequality reduction, and economic inclusion.
